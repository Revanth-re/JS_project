Catering Services We Offer

Corporate Catering
We provide high-quality catering services for business meetings, conferences, and other corporate events. Options include breakfast buffets, lunch buffets, and full-service catering for large meetings.
Event Catering
From weddings to corporate banquets, we offer customized catering services for any type of event. Our team provides everything from appetizers to full-course meals, designed to suit your occasion.
Private Catering
If you're hosting an intimate gathering or a private dinner, we provide a range of options, including personal chef services and customizable menus that fit your needs.
Specialized Catering
We offer specialized menus that cater to various dietary restrictions such as vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, halal, and kosher. Your guests' dietary needs are our priority.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How far in advance should I book my catering service?
We recommend booking at least 2-4 weeks in advance to ensure availability and menu customization.
Can I make dietary requests for my guests?
Yes, we can accommodate special dietary needs such as vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, and allergen-free meals. Please inform us ahead of time.
Do you provide staff for events?
Yes, we offer professional staff including chefs, servers, and event coordinators. Staffing costs may vary depending on the size and type of event.
Can I choose my own menu?
Absolutely! You can work with our catering experts to customize your menu based on your preferences and dietary requirements.
What happens if I need to cancel my order?
Cancellations can be made up to 48 hours before the event with a full refund. After that, a 50% cancellation fee applies.
