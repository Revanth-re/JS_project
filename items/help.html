Help & Support

Welcome to our help page. We're here to assist with any issues you may experience using our catering services platform. If your issue isn't listed, please contact us.

🖥️ Server & Technical Issues

1. The website is slow or not loading?

This may be due to temporary server maintenance. Please try refreshing or check back later. If the issue persists for more than 15 minutes, contact our support team.

2. I can't log in or sign up?

Make sure you are connected to the internet and your credentials are correct. If you forgot your password, use the "Forgot Password" option or contact support for account recovery.

🍽️ Food & Catering Concerns

1. My order didn’t arrive or was late.

We’re sorry to hear that. Please contact the service provider directly via their profile or report the issue to us with your order ID.

2. The food was not as described or of poor quality.

You can leave a review for the caterer and also report the issue via our platform. We take food quality seriously and will investigate any reports.

💳 Payment & Billing Issues

1. I was charged but didn’t get confirmation.

Sometimes confirmation emails are delayed. Check your spam/junk folders. If you still haven’t received anything after 10 minutes, please contact us with proof of payment.

2. My payment failed.

Please ensure your card details are correct and that your bank supports online transactions. You can also try a different payment method or reach out to support.

🤝 More About Us

We’re passionate about connecting people with reliable, high-quality catering services in their area. Our platform supports both individual and corporate events, offering a wide range of menus and services. With our growing network of verified caterers, we aim to make every meal memorable.

We value transparency, food safety, and customer satisfaction above all. If something goes wrong, we work quickly to make it right.
